It is a terminal app for saving media from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and X. It supports video, audio, batches, playlists/profiles where available, cookies for login-gated links, and Premiere-friendly MP4 conversion.

## Thread

1/ I built 4Saves, a terminal media downloader for creators and editors.

2/ It supports YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X/Twitter, and other sites supported by yt-dlp.

3/ The goal is simple: paste a link, choose what you want, and get a usable file without remembering a bunch of yt-dlp flags.

4/ It can download videos, audio, batches, playlists/profiles where supported, thumbnails, metadata, and subtitles.

5/ Videos are converted to H.264/AAC MP4 by default, so they are easier to import into Premiere and play in QuickTime.

6/ It is open source:
https://github.com/mofe404/4saves
